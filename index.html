Source integrity

Why these dates matter

Bond markets react most sharply when the expected path of inflation, policy rates or sovereign supply changes. This calendar prioritizes events with a direct transmission channel into yields, curves, breakevens and term premium.

Data policy. Dates are sourced from official institutions. “Impact” and “Why it matters” are BondStats analytical labels. If a source is temporarily unavailable, the collector preserves the latest valid data for that source.

A calendar built for fixed income

FOMC, ECB, Bank of England and Bank of Japan decisions sit beside U.S. CPI, Nonfarm Payrolls and Treasury funding events because they influence different parts of the same pricing chain.

Live schedule, persistent context

The calendar is refreshed automatically rather than maintained as a static article. Future versions can add consensus, actual releases, auction tails, bid-to-cover ratios and post-event yield reactions.
